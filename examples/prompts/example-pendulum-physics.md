A simple pendulum of length L = 0.80 m is suspended from a fixed support. The bob has mass m = 0.25 kg. At rest the pendulum hangs vertically. The bob is then pulled aside so the string makes an angle θ₀ = 30° with the vertical and is released from rest.

(a) Draw free-body diagrams of the bob at the release point and at the lowest point of the swing. Label the tension T and the weight mg on each diagram.

(b) Using conservation of energy, find the speed of the bob at the bottom of the swing.

(c) Find the tension in the string at the bottom of the swing.

Assume g = 9.8 m/s². Neglect air resistance. The string is massless and inextensible.
